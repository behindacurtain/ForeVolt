ForeVolt (“the App”) is developed and maintained by **Gage Dunstan** (“we”, “us”, “our”).  
This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact: **gagedunstan@gmail.com**

## ForeVolt 1.1: accounts and purchases

This section applies when you use ForeVolt 1.1. ForeVolt 1.1 does not offer ForeVolt sign-up or sign-in, and does not use Firebase for ForeVolt accounts or Pro access. Apple handles payments. ForeVolt uses Apple’s StoreKit to check access to Pro from a Lifetime purchase or an eligible existing subscription. Restoring a purchase does not create a ForeVolt account.

If you have a question about a ForeVolt account from an earlier version, contact ForeVolt support at the address above.

## Earlier ForeVolt versions, including 1.0

The original notice below describes the account and billing features of earlier versions.

## 1. Summary

- ForeVolt uses **Firebase Authentication** to let you create an account and sign in.
- ForeVolt uses **Apple’s StoreKit** to manage subscriptions and free trials.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- We do **not** run third-party advertising in the App.
- Where possible, battery simulations run locally on your device.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### A) Account information (Firebase Authentication)
If you sign up or sign in, we may process:
- Email address
- Firebase user identifier (UID)
- Authentication metadata (e.g., sign-in timestamps)

This data is used to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Let you sign in securely
- Support account recovery and account deletion requests

### B) Subscription and purchase information (Apple StoreKit)
Subscriptions, trials, and payment processing are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment details.

We may access limited purchase/entitlement information via StoreKit to determine:
- Whether you have an active subscription
- Whether you are currently in a trial
- Whether you are entitled to Pro features

### C) Usage data
ForeVolt does not intentionally collect analytics for advertising or tracking.

If you contact support, we will receive whatever information you choose to include in your email.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use information only to:
- Provide access to the App and its features
- Enforce feature access (e.g., Demo vs Trial vs Pro)
- Maintain account security
- Provide support and respond to requests
- Comply with legal obligations where required

---

## 4. Data storage and retention

- Account data is stored and processed using **Firebase Authentication**.
- Trial/PDF export limits may be stored locally on your device using system storage (e.g., UserDefaults) to enforce trial rules.
- If/when cloud saving features are introduced (e.g., saved vehicles, simulations, reports), we will update this policy to describe what is stored and where.

We retain account data for as long as your account exists, unless deletion is requested.

---

## 5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your information.

We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the App:
- **Google Firebase** (authentication services)
- **Apple** (subscriptions and payments via StoreKit)

We may disclose information if required by law.

---

## 6. Your choices and rights

### Account access
- You can sign in/out within the App.
- You can request account deletion using the in-app “Delete account” flow (where available).

### Deleting your account
When you delete your account:
- Your authentication record will be removed.
- If you later add cloud saving, deletion will also remove saved vehicles, simulations, and reports (where applicable).

If you need help deleting your account, contact: **gagedunstan@gmail.com**

---

## 7. Children’s privacy
ForeVolt is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us to request deletion.
